THE REALITY CHECK

Recognise the story a game is selling.

01 / THE STREAK

“I'm due a win.”

In these independent-round models, a losing streak doesn't change the next round's odds. Mines tiles within one board are different: the chance changes as safe tiles are removed.

02 / THE NEAR MISS

“I was so close.”

Two matching symbols still return zero in our Slots model. A near miss is a loss, not evidence that the next result will match.

03 / THE RECOVERY

“I'll win it back.”

A larger stake increases the amount at risk. It does not reverse the house advantage. Doubling stakes can create very large losses within a short sequence.

Read the mathematics and model assumptions

Expected net = stake × (probability of success × total-return multiplier − 1). Our Mines and Crash models return 97% in expectation; Dice returns 98%; Slots returns 95%. Red on single-zero Roulette returns 36/37. Commercial games may use different rules and paytables.

Mines: P(k safe tiles) = ∏(25 − m − i)/(25 − i), for i from 0 to k−1. Return = 0.97/P. Crash: a random U in [0,1) sets the stop point to max(1, 0.97/(1−U)). For a chosen target t ≥ 1.1, P(reach t) = 0.97/t. Slots: three independent reels with six equally likely symbols; six matching triples among 216 outcomes return 34.2×.

Browser randomness is used for learning; this is not a certified gambling system. No strategy here predicts outcomes on another website.

TAKE THE CONVERSATION FURTHER

A lab for learning.
Not a reason to gamble.

Created for youth awareness by Ramprasad Atluri. For younger learners, use the experiment and discussion with a teacher or trusted adult.

A 20-minute classroom activity

  1. Predict · 3 minutes. Does a 49% success rate with a 2× total return break even?
  2. Observe · 5 minutes. Compare ten outcomes in two models. Record stake, return, and net result.
  3. Zoom out · 5 minutes. Run the experiment. Why can observed and expected results differ?
  4. Reflect · 7 minutes. Discuss near misses, selective win screenshots, and why “guaranteed profit” claims deserve scrutiny.

If play stops feeling like a choice

Step away and talk to a trusted adult, friend, teacher, or qualified support professional. Avoid borrowing or trying to recover losses through more play.

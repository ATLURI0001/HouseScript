What can this prove — and what can't it?

This audit proves what your own demo server reported. It is not a cryptographically independent attestation. A dishonest operator could fabricate both a result and an audit log. A screenshot or a streak alone cannot prove fairness or manipulation.

Ask whether rules and paytables were declared in advance, whether independent testing applies, whether a claimed verification scheme can be independently checked, and whether withdrawals and complaints are handled transparently. A provable-fairness claim does not guarantee payment or remove the house edge.

Great Britain's Gambling Commission RTS 7 requires acceptably random outcomes and prohibits adaptive outcome changes and fabricated near misses within its scope. Rules vary by jurisdiction. Read RTS 7 ↗
